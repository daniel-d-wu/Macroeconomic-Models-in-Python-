# MacroFinance-Models-in-Python-

In the Financial Economics literature, the "equity premium puzzle" refers to a mismatch between standard neoclassical economic models and the observed US equity return (roughly 7% over bonds) over the past 70~ years. This concept was introduced by Mehra and Prescott (1985), where they show the average observed equity premium can be rationalized only with an incredulously high level of risk aversion. Since then, this puzzle has spawned a line of research that borrow calibration techniques to explain why we observe a 6% equity premium at the aggregate economy level. Two major competing explanations are Long Run Risk (Bansal and Yaron 2004) and Rare Disasters (Barro, 2006, 2009). In this file, I replicate the approach taken by Mehra and Prescott (1985) and Bansal and Yaron (2004).
